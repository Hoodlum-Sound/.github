Hey, you found me!

Welcome to Hoodlum Sound, the workshop where my creative and personal coding projects live. 
It's not a company, it's not a startup, and there's no roadmap with swim lanes. 
Think of it as a wizard's workbench: half-finished spellbooks, 
a few tools that actually work, and a lot of "huh, what happens if I do this?"

Everything here is made for the love of making things.

## The vibe
**Playful first.** If it's not at least a little fun, why are we doing it?
Art and engineering are friends. Thirty-odd years of building networks taught me how things work. 
Poetry and photography taught me why they matter.
Messy is allowed. 
Some repos are polished. Some are a bard's first draft. Both are welcome.

Feel free to browse, fork, borrow, or remix. If something here helps you out, that's a win. If you find a bug, open an issue and be kind about it.

Most things are shared in the spirit of take what's useful, leave the rest. Check each repo's license for the specifics.

## Say hello
Got a question, a cool idea, or a "wait, how did you do that?" Open an issue or a discussion on whichever repo fits. 
I read them all, and I'm usually caffeinated.
